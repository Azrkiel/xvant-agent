## 14. Addendum 2026-10-08: Opus planner benchmarked

Written by Claude (Opus 5.5) in Claude Code. The operator asked for the Opus runs, then for the two harness problems to be fixed, the records kept as they were, and everything committed and pushed.

**Result.** `claude-opus-haiku-xvant` on `v1`, 1 repeat: **23/24 accepted**, 12/12 held-out, 4/4 parallel-shaped, median 145 s, none incomplete. Compare 67/72 (single Haiku, median 31 s) and 55/72 (Haiku-planned). No plan was rejected and no attempt failed the hidden check. Report: `docs/evidence/benchmark-v1-opus-haiku-r1.json` (168 records: the 144 earlier ones plus these 24). One repeat is directional. The assumption in §9 that Opus produces valid plans held on this suite; the one about larger tasks is still untested.

**The one failure** is `diagnose-off-by-one`: `STALE_FENCE` right after two nodes were dispatched, on a valid plan. The attempt's store lease (30 s) lapsed, which needs the benchmark process to miss its 10 s heartbeats for over 30 s. Cause not found; the host was short of memory all session, so a stall is my guess. It stays a failure in the records because its workers did launch.

**What changed in code** (`7f56e7c`):

- `WorkerSpec.model`; `orchestratedConfiguration({ plannerModel })` makes worker 1 planner and reviewer on that model and workers 2 and 3 plain workers on the runtime's model. `scripts/benchmark.mjs` maps `claude-opus-haiku` to Haiku workers with an Opus planner.
- `CampaignStopped`: a turn refused with `VERSION_UNSUPPORTED` ends the campaign and leaves the attempt without a record, so a resume runs it.
- `HostInterrupted`: `STALE_FENCE` is recorded as `incomplete` with a `host:` reason. Benchmark attempt stores take a five-minute lease. This is a mitigation, not a fix for whatever stalled the process.
